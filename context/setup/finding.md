Finding things takes three commands:

    ws search march report             these words, across everything
    ws search documents march report   these words, in one kind
    ws list people                     everything of one kind

Search gives you REFs, and `ws show REF` prints an object with its neighbours, each
of them another REF to show. So an agent starts at one thing and walks along the
relations to what matters: from a project to its papers, from a paper to its authors,
from an author to their institute. It finds what it needs without reading the whole
workspace or guessing at folders. This is why relations matter as much as the records.

There is more than this, and none of it needs memorising:

    ws capabilities      every command there is
    ws help              the manual
    ws help projects     the manual on one kind
    ws describe COMMAND  what one command does and what it touches
