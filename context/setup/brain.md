This is a second brain: one folder that holds your projects, tasks, documents,
papers, contacts and career facts, in order and connected. Its shape is a knowledge
graph: things, and the relations between them.

The idea follows Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki. Rather than digging through raw
material for every question, an agent builds a lasting, linked store of what you
know, and that store keeps growing between conversations. ws takes this from pages
of notes to a graph of small typed records.

Two kinds of work meet here. ws is deterministic: a collection of small programs
that do the same thing every time. Agents work in latent space: they read, judge
and write, and no two runs are alike. The brain itself is only data. It is the
memory they share.

So the rule is that the workspace grows only through ws. You and your agents use
the same commands, and agents are told never to touch the records any other way.
That is what keeps them from going rogue. Whichever agent works on it, and however
many, it grows the same way and stays in order.
