The person object is the private side. Everyone you deal with gets a person
record; this one is labelled as the owner, so that agents can find it. Facts
that do not belong on a CV go here, such as your other addresses and what to
call you. Relations attach to it later: the papers you co-authored, the
organisations you belong to, the events you attended.

Each fact lives in one place, so nothing can drift: what a CV may show stays in
your identity, and the rest goes here. Agents read both with `ws owner`.
