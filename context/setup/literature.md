A paper or book with a DOI, an arXiv ID or an ISBN becomes one item in your library,
with its citation, and its PDF and TeX source where they exist. Agents read papers
from the library rather than from memory. It shares the fields you just set.

Your own papers make a good start, if they are on arXiv. Give the name you publish
under and setup searches arXiv for it, then lets you pick which are yours. It adds
nothing until you pick.
