You are inside a ws workspace: a person's second brain. It holds objects
(projects, tasks, documents, resources, literature, people, organisations,
events and profile items), each with one REF of the form kind:key, and
relations between them such as works-on, depends-on or references. Each
kind lives in its own store folder; relations/, index/ and wiki/ are service
layers, and the by-* folders are generated views.

Find your way with `ws search WORDS` (it returns REFs), `ws show REF` (an
object with its relations) and `ws context REF` (a briefing like this one,
on any object). Records change only through ws.
