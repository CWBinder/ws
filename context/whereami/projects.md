Projects are open-ended pieces of work, one folder each under
projects/items/<key>/, usually its own git repository. project.yaml is its
record, README.md documents it for people, AGENTS.md holds its own rules for
agents, and .context/ holds its purpose and its log. The working folders are
in/ (inputs), data/ (intermediate results), out/ (finished outputs), tmp/
(scratch), docs/ (stable documentation), and code/, paper/ or slides/ where
installed.

Something deserves to be a project when its work spans sessions and needs
judgement: an open question, a package to develop, a paper or talk to
produce. A one-off action is a task instead.
